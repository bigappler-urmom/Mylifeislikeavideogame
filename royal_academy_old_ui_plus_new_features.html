Research rank guide

E — Starter: identifies the question and gathers basic information.

D — Developing: explains key ideas in your own words.

C — Capable: uses credible sources and clear reasoning.

B — Strong: compares evidence and addresses limitations.

A — Excellent: develops a thoughtful, well-supported conclusion.

S — Exceptional: demonstrates depth, originality, and careful evaluation.

Ranks should reflect the quality of the work, not how long you spend on it.
